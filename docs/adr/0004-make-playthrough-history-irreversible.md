# Make Playthrough History Irreversible

A Playthrough is an irreversible Committed History: it supports exact replay and continuation, but not rewind, save replacement, or choosing again from an earlier decision. Players explore alternatives by starting another Playthrough. Initial generation may vary even for the same inputs, while accepted events and presentation remain immutable afterward; this gives choices durable weight and keeps completion evidence causally sound, at the cost of giving up traditional route-navigation saves.
