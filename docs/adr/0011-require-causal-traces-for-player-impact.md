# Require Causal Traces for Player Impact

A Player Choice counts as influential only when its committed Choice Meaning is referenced by an accepted Story Consequence and visibly carried into later state or dramatic development; important choices must also be available to the Dynamic Ending and Dramatic Review. Different wording or nondeterministic output alone is not evidence of impact. Automated strategies and human playtests evaluate these Causal Traces, making player agency auditable at the cost of requiring explicit causal references throughout the runtime.
