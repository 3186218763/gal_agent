# Constrain Drama With Obligations, Not Fixed Beats

Authors specify Dramatic Obligations that must be meaningfully addressed, but they do not prescribe a fixed sequence of scenes or plot beats. The Agent chooses when, where, and how each obligation appears and whether it is fulfilled, broken, transformed, or deliberately left open; early convergence is unavailable while significant obligations remain unaddressed. This preserves authorial dramatic intent without rebuilding a route tree, at the cost of needing explicit obligation lifecycle and payoff evidence.
